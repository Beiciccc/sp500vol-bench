Apparent gains from learned text representations over a strong structured baseline are, we show, largely an artefact of weak baselines and entity-identity leakage---a failure mode that disclosure-NLP evaluations do not control for---and we ship the tooling to detect and remove it. \textbf{SP500Vol-Bench} is a survivorship-free, point-in-time benchmark linking 144{,}129 SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K) from S\&P~500 constituents over 2010--2025 to forward realised volatility at horizons of 5, 10, and 20 trading days (431{,}245 aligned filing-by-horizon rows; a successor-CIK recovery step restores $\sim$2{,}000 filings from acquired or delisted firms). Alongside it we release a reusable \emph{incremental-value} protocol that credits text only for the out-of-sample QLIKE it removes beyond a \emph{recalibrated} price reference---adjudicated by day-clustered Diebold--Mariano tests, Holm correction, moving-block bootstrap, and label-shuffle and within-date placebos, and bracketed by a \emph{reference interval} (a single-HAR upper bound to a firm-identity lower bound) together with a five-model maximal price pool. The protocol's first output is a rigorous, placebo-confirmed near-null. No text or fusion model beats HAR-RV out of sample (0 of 180). A small increment against a single recalibrated-HAR reference (38 of 69 cells on the seed-ensemble primary; 29 under a single seed; 0.2--5.9\% QLIKE) does not survive the controls: a \emph{zero-text} firm-mean forecast reproduces a \emph{larger} apparent gain in 53 of 69 cells, the maximal price pool absorbs the remainder, and the two survivor sets are disjoint---no cell clears both. What remains is a modest $+$0.2--0.5\% in a handful of event-driven 8-K cells, cross-sectional (which-firm) rather than regime-timing (when), with no portfolio-level economic value; across a spectrum from bag-of-words through fine-tuned encoders and frozen 7--8B LLM embeddings to a prompted 32B LLM, prompting extracts more of this thin signal than pooling same-lineage embeddings, yet all trail price. SP500Vol-Bench supplies the survivorship-free, leakage-free, control-rich protocol that makes such artefacts visible and removable.
