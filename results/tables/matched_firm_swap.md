# Matched-firm text swap — separating identity from content

Within each day, firms are paired by nearest validation-period mean RV and their text forecasts swapped (identity/level channel preserved, document-firm correspondence destroyed). Retention = swapped/real increment. Identity-shortcut cells retain (~1); content cells die (~0). Random within-date permutation shown for triangulation.

**Genuine cells (n=38)**: median retention **0.31** (long-form 0.33 over 24; event-driven 0.17 over 14).
