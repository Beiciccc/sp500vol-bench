## RESTATED vs ORIGINAL

| quantity | ORIGINAL (text over recalibrated A2-HAR, no firm term) | RESTATED (reference = [1, log HAR, log firm-mean-val-RV]; day-clustered DM) |
|---|---|---|
| firms with val rows (rest use global val mean) | — | **62.9%** of firms; **91.5%** (long_form) / **91.9%** (event_driven) of TEST rows |
| cells where text still adds over the firm-augmented reference | most (A2-only) | **14/69** raw, **8/69** after Holm |
| long_form cells flipping NEGATIVE (text HURTS) | ~0 | **40/45** |
| firm-mean-only (zero-text) beats plain f_R (reproduces the increment) | — | **53/69** cells; mean rel +0.52% |


**Bottom line (honest):** once firm identity (the firm's own mean validation RV) enters the reference, the incremental value of disclosure text largely disappears. Text survives in only **8/69** cells after Holm; in long_form **40/45** cells flip NEGATIVE (text HURTS relative to the firm-augmented reference). A zero-text forecast that adds ONLY the firm mean to the HAR reference reproduces a comparable improvement over plain f_R in **53/69** cells (mean +0.52%), showing that much of the original 'text increment' was firm-identity confounding, not disclosure content. The residual signal concentrates in event_driven 8-K cells (long_form mean rel% firm = -2.25%, event_driven = -0.10%).
