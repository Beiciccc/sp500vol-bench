\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The firm-identity reference is a deliberately strong control. A firm's mean validation-period volatility absorbs \emph{any} firm-stable signal---including legitimately persistent firm-level risk language that a text model may capture honestly. We therefore report the full reference interval rather than a single verdict, and read the identity-controlled result as the lower bound of text's credit: what survives it is the \emph{filing-specific} component. The four-specification identity battery (Holm survivors between 8 and 14 of 69 across specifications) shows this reading is not an artefact of one identity construction, but the control cannot by itself distinguish ``text proxies firm identity'' from ``text carries persistent firm information the reference happens to encode''.

Second, the headline counts are convention-sensitive: under variance-unit rather than volatility-unit QLIKE, 38 of 69 genuine cells against the single recalibrated-HAR reference become 20 of 69. We disclose the convention wherever a count appears and report both. Third, the cross-family non-replication is capability-confounded: Yi-1.5-34B and Phi-4-14B are smaller and older than Qwen3-32B and mode-collapse ($\geq$73\% of forecasts on a single value), so we can state only that the residual \emph{does not replicate} across the families tested, not that it is family-specific.

Fourth, the contamination controls bound memorisation but cannot prove its absence: date-plus-ticker prompts with zero text reproduce 31--77\% of the full-text increment in the three well-identified cells, and although the residual remains significant in 5 of 6 cells (Holm) after the training-cutoff era split, that split is coarse---a single cutoff with a short post-cutoff window. Fifth, our controls operationalise \emph{firm} identity only; author identity and document-source identity are not operationalised beyond the disclosure-type split. Sixth, the benchmark covers one market, capitalisation tier, and disclosure regime (US large-cap SEC filings), and the near-null is scoped to this instance, not to disclosure NLP in general.

Finally, the surviving event-driven 8-K residual is small ($+$0.2--0.5\% QLIKE), prompting-specific and non-replicating across the LLM families tested, cross-sectional rather than regime-timing, and below economic materiality (median $\Delta$Sharpe $+$0.003, significant in 1 of 18 cells---approximately the multiplicity false-positive floor); it also collapses under a deployable expanding-window combiner. We report it as a characterised residual, not a deployment recommendation.
